Privacy Policy

Rclone accesses my Google Drive using permissions I authorize through Google OAuth.

Google Drive data is used for personal file access, transfers, and synchronization.

This website does not receive or store my Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Data Sharing

I do not sell Google user data or share it for advertising purposes.

Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
